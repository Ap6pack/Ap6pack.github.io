# Security contact for ap6pack.github.io, including the Sundew honeypot
# dataset published at /dist/honeypot/ and its indicator feed at
# /dist/honeypot/data/v1/feed/.
#
# Two different things arrive here, and both are welcome:
#
#   1. A vulnerability in this site or in the dashboard.
#   2. A request to remove an address from the indicator feed.
#
# On (2): appearing in the feed means traffic reached a honeypot from that
# address. It does not mean the address's owner did anything wrong - a large
# share of honeypot traffic comes from compromised third parties, and
# residential addresses get reassigned. Removal requests are honoured. Include
# the address and a rough time window; there is no process to exhaust.
